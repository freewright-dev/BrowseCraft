! YouTube ad rules, ported from the uBlock Origin / EasyList YouTube notes.
!
! The video pre-roll/mid-roll ads themselves are removed by the YouTube
! "scriptlet" in the injected page script (adblock.rs, YT_SCRIPTLET): it prunes
! adPlacements / playerAds / adSlots from the player data, like uBO's
! `set-constant`, `json-prune-fetch-response` and `json-prune-xhr-response`.
! These filters cover the page furniture and the ad-tracking requests.
!
! Deliberately NOT blocked: googlevideo.com, /youtubei/v1/player and
! /videoplayback. They carry the video you asked for.
